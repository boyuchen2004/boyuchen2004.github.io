I am Boyu Chen (陈博俣), a first-year PhD student at MMLab, The University of Hong Kong, advised by Prof. [Ping Luo](https://luoping.me/), Prof. [Shenghua Gao](https://datascience.hku.hk/people/shenghua-gao/), and Prof. [Yi Ma](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~yima/).

Previously, I studied Automation at Tsinghua University.

From general intelligence to physical intelligence
======
My research aims to bring **general intelligence to physical intelligence**: enabling generalist models to perceive precisely and act across different bodies. I study two complementary foundations: **action representation**, which connects human and robot experience for policy learning and world modeling, and **precise perception**, which grounds decisions in the physical world.
